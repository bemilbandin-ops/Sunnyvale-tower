# Unified cartoon art direction

This rework removes the active runtime dependence on the mixed painterly sprite sheets and replaces the visible game world with one consistent cartoon system.

## Visual rules

- Near-black outlines on every important gameplay object.
- Five-to-eight pixel line weights at the 1280×720 reference resolution.
- Flat color blocks with one simple highlight or shadow shape.
- Simplified, slightly exaggerated character proportions.
- Shared palette across characters, vehicle, decks, weapons, enemies, UI, and scenery.
- Backgrounds use lower contrast and thinner line work than gameplay objects.
- No photo textures or realistic sprite crops in the active runtime.

## Palette

- Ink: `#17120f`
- Sky: `#a8d9e4`
- Cream: `#f4e4b9`
- Rust: `#b85331`
- Mustard: `#dfb542`
- Metal: `#66787b`
- Green: `#55785b`

The Ricky, Bubbles, and Julian SVG portraits follow the supplied reference: large heads, bold outlines, simplified faces, flatter cel shading, and clean costume silhouettes.
