How the stack works

Separate health

Every deck has its own HP bar and independently mounted weapon.

Targeted attacks

Runners and bruisers hit the bottom. Raccoons climb. Bottles hit random decks.

Collapse

When a deck breaks, every deck above it crashes down, takes impact damage, and stops firing briefly.

Roadside building

Collect six scrap to add the third deck, replace weapons, reinforce, or repair.
